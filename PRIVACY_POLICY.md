## 1. Introduction

Prometheus Studio ("Prometheus Studio," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how **Prometheus Studio**, which operates the Prometheus Studio AI-powered video editing platform, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data when you use `prometheusstudio.tech`, our applications, and related services.

We seek to comply with applicable privacy and data-protection laws, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and comparable laws where they apply. This policy does not reduce any non-waivable rights you have under those laws.

## 3. Personal Data We Collect

### Account and profile data

We collect account information such as your name, email address, encrypted or hashed authentication credentials, profile details, account preferences, notification settings, workspace details, and connected-account information needed to provide a feature you enable.

### Payment and subscription data

Dodo Payments handles full payment-card numbers, CVV/CVC codes, and bank-account details. Prometheus Studio does not store those credentials. We may receive limited supplier-side transaction information from Dodo Payments, such as transaction or subscription identifiers, payment and subscription status, plan, currency, amount, billing history, invoice or receipt links, and limited billing details needed for support, accounting, fraud prevention, and Service access.

### Usage, device, and technical data

We collect log, device, browser, operating-system, IP-address, session, feature-usage, error, performance, security, and diagnostic information. We may collect this data automatically through cookies, local storage, SDKs, and similar technologies.

### Content data

We process videos, images, audio, text, prompts, captions, transcripts, project files, timelines, edits, exports, and AI-generated outputs that you upload, create, or direct us to process. This data is necessary to provide storage, editing, AI transformation, rendering, export, and user-selected integrations.

### Communications data

We collect support tickets, emails, chat messages, feedback, survey responses, and other communications you send us.

## 5. AI and ML Processing

Prometheus Studio uses AI and machine-learning systems to generate or modify video content, assist editing workflows, create captions or effects, and support rendering. Your Content may be transmitted to and processed by our AI and cloud providers when you invoke these features.

AI outputs are generated algorithmically and may be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, unsafe, non-unique, or unsuitable for your purpose. You are responsible for reviewing outputs before use or publication.

We may use de-identified and aggregated usage data to improve our Service and AI systems. We do not use your private Content to train models made available to other users without your explicit consent. We retain rights in our underlying AI technology, but do not claim ownership of your private Content or outputs merely because you used the Platform.

## 6. How We Share Personal Data

We share personal data only as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

- **Dodo Payments:** We share transaction-context data, account email, and billing details needed to begin and support checkout. Dodo Payments independently processes payment, tax, invoice, fraud, refund, and chargeback data as Merchant of Record.
- **Cloud, hosting, storage, and delivery providers:** We use providers such as AWS, Cloudflare, and Supabase to host the Platform, authenticate users, store data, process media, and deliver content. They act as service providers/processors where applicable and process data under contractual safeguards.
- **AI, analytics, monitoring, and support providers:** We may use carefully selected vendors to provide AI processing, analytics, diagnostics, communications, and customer support. They receive only the data needed for their service and are bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations where applicable.
- **Connected platforms:** When you choose to connect a storage or publishing service, we share the Content and metadata you instruct us to transmit. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.
- **Legal, security, and corporate transactions:** We may disclose data to comply with law, enforce our rights, protect users or the public, investigate abuse, or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by California law without the notice and choice required by law.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, encryption or equivalent protections at rest where appropriate, access controls, authentication, least-privilege practices, logging, backups, and incident-response processes. No service or internet transmission is completely secure. Keep your credentials confidential and report suspected account compromise to **support@prometheusstudio.tech**.

## 9. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and security needs.

- Account data is generally retained while the account is active and for the applicable limitation period plus up to 2 months, unless a longer period is legally required.
- Content data is retained while your account is active and is scheduled for deletion within 30 days after account deletion, unless we must retain it for law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, backups, or you ask us to preserve it.
- Payment-status and accounting data in our systems may be retained for the period required by tax and accounting laws, commonly 6 to 7 years depending on the jurisdiction.
- De-identified or aggregated data may be retained longer when it can no longer reasonably identify you.

Backups are deleted on a separate rolling schedule. We will not restore deleted Content from backups except where operationally necessary and permitted by law.
