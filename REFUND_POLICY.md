# Prometheus Studio Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** 14 July 2026

> **Review note:** This policy is a product-specific draft and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before publication. It does not limit non-waivable consumer rights.

## 1. Overview

Prometheus Studio provides immediately accessible digital services, including cloud-based video editing, AI processing, media storage, rendering, and export. Dodo Payments is the Merchant of Record and authorized reseller for paid transactions. Dodo Payments processes payments, applicable taxes, receipts or invoices, chargebacks, and approved payment refunds.

Refund requests are subject to this policy, Dodo Payments' Buyer Terms, payment-network rules, fraud controls, and applicable consumer-protection law. This policy explains Prometheus Studio's support-review process; it does not override Dodo Payments' independent refund decisions or statutory rights.

## 2. When a Refund May Be Considered

We generally consider a request submitted within 7 days of the transaction date when one of the following applies:

- a verified technical failure prevented meaningful access to paid Service features and our support team could not reasonably resolve it;
- a duplicate charge, billing error, or unauthorized charge is verified after investigation;
- the purchased Service was not delivered substantially as described, subject to review of the account, transaction, and available remedy; or
- a refund is required by applicable law.

The 7-day period is Prometheus Studio's support-review window. It is not a Dodo Payments-mandated deadline and does not limit rights that cannot legally be limited.

## 3. When a Refund Is Normally Not Available

Except where required by law or approved after review, refunds are not normally available for:

- a change of mind after Service access, digital delivery, or transmission has begun;
- dissatisfaction with an AI output's aesthetic quality, accuracy, uniqueness, or suitability, because AI generation is inherently variable;
- failure to use the Service during a paid period;
- unused time after subscription cancellation;
- an account terminated or restricted for a Terms or Acceptable Use Policy violation;
- fraud, refund abuse, chargeback misuse, manipulative conduct, or material misrepresentation; or
- significant use of paid Service resources, including extensive AI generation, rendering, export, or storage. Usage exceeding 50% of the applicable plan's measured limits will generally weigh against a refund, but we will consider the facts and mandatory consumer rights in each case.

Promotional or discounted purchases are reviewed using the amount actually paid. We do not provide refunds based on a non-discounted list price.

## 4. Digital Delivery and Consumer Rights

By purchasing, you request immediate provision of digital services. Where local law permits, you acknowledge that a withdrawal right may be lost after digital delivery or transmission begins. This does not waive rights that cannot be waived under applicable law. If you believe you have a statutory cancellation or withdrawal right, tell us your location and the basis of your request when contacting support.

## 5. How to Request a Refund

1. Submit a request to **support@prometheusstudio.tech** or use the Dodo Payments buyer-support flow linked in your payment receipt.
2. Include the account email, invoice or order number, transaction date, and a clear reason for the request. Do not send card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials to Prometheus Studio.
3. We review the subscription and transaction status, delivery and support records, account usage, billing history, and any applicable Dodo Payments requirements.
4. We aim to communicate our support decision within 5 to 7 business days. Complex fraud, technical, or payment investigations may take longer.
5. If a refund is approved, Dodo Payments issues it to the original payment method. The time for funds to appear depends on Dodo Payments and your bank, card issuer, or wallet provider and is commonly 5 to 10 business days.

## 6. Cancellation Is Not a Refund

Cancellation stops a future subscription renewal. Unless required by law or otherwise confirmed in writing, your paid access continues through the current paid period and no partial refund is issued for unused time. A refund returns a prior payment and is reviewed separately.

Cancel through **support@prometheusstudio.tech**. Where possible, request cancellation at least 48 hours before renewal. This operational guidance does not limit a right that cannot be limited by law.

## 7. Annual Plans, Pre-Orders, and Taxes

For annual plans, a pro-rated refund may be considered case by case when requested within 30 days of purchase and where usage review supports it. We do not promise a pro-rated annual refund unless required by law.

If we offer a pre-order, you may request a full refund before digital delivery begins. After delivery, the standard policy applies.

If indirect tax (such as VAT, GST, or sales tax) was charged and you are eligible for a tax adjustment or refund, contact Dodo Payments with the documentation requested in its Buyer Terms. Dodo Payments, not Prometheus Studio, handles transaction tax adjustments.

## 8. Chargebacks and Disputes

Contact Prometheus Studio and Dodo Payments before opening a chargeback or bank dispute so that we can investigate and attempt to resolve the issue. This request does not limit your lawful right to dispute a charge.

Chargeback abuse or fraud may result in account termination and a prohibition on future use of the Service. Under Dodo Payments' Buyer Terms, Dodo Payments may, at its sole discretion, apply remedies to a meritless chargeback, including USD 100 in liquidated damages. Prometheus Studio does not impose that Dodo Payments remedy.

If you disagree with our support decision, reply to **support@prometheusstudio.tech** with any additional material. We aim to complete a further review within 10 business days. Unresolved disputes are governed by the dispute terms in the Terms of Service and applicable law.
